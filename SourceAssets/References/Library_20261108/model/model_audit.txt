3. Existing exterior window spacing and block bounds cannot be treated as real room dimensions. Do not anchor supposedly exact new interior features to the facade's repeated panes without registration evidence.
4. A proposed total floor area, cabinet specification, or photo with a few visible walls cannot alone establish room aspect ratio/perimeter or which exterior face is shown. Treat proposals separately from as-built photos.
